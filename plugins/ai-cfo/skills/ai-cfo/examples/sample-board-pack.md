# Sample Board Finance Pack

## CFO Executive Summary
- Revenue grew through Q2, but gross margin compressed as COGS grew faster than sales.
- EBITDA declined from the February peak despite continued revenue growth.
- Cash declined while receivables and inventory increased, indicating working-capital pressure.
- Near-term focus should be collections, inventory discipline, and cost control.
- Forecast scenarios should test whether current growth can be funded without additional capital.

## Decisions
1. Approve collections recovery plan for overdue accounts.
2. Review marketing growth against contribution margin.
3. Set a minimum cash buffer for expansion decisions.
