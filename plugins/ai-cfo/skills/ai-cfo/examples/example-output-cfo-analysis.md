# Example CFO Analysis

## Executive Summary
- Revenue is growing, but gross margin and EBITDA are weakening.
- Receivables and inventory have grown faster than the available cash balance.
- The business appears to be absorbing more cash into working capital as it grows.
- Collections and inventory should be reviewed before accelerating discretionary spend.
- A rolling cash forecast is required before making a financing or expansion decision.

## Key Questions for Management
1. What caused the increase in COGS as a percentage of revenue?
2. Why did AR increase faster than revenue?
3. Is inventory growth strategic, seasonal, or slow-moving?
4. Are customer collection terms changing?
5. What is the minimum operating cash buffer?
