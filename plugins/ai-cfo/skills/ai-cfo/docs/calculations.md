# Finance Calculation Standards

Use these formulas unless the business context requires a different definition. State any modified definition.

## Profitability
- Gross profit = Revenue - Cost of goods sold
- Gross margin % = Gross profit / Revenue
- EBITDA = EBIT + Depreciation + Amortisation
- EBITDA margin % = EBITDA / Revenue
- Net margin % = Net income / Revenue
- Contribution margin = Revenue - Variable costs
- Contribution margin % = Contribution margin / Revenue

## Liquidity and leverage
- Current ratio = Current assets / Current liabilities
- Quick ratio = (Cash + Marketable securities + Accounts receivable) / Current liabilities
- Debt to equity = Interest-bearing debt / Equity
- Net debt = Interest-bearing debt - Cash and cash equivalents
- Net debt / EBITDA = Net debt / EBITDA, only when EBITDA is meaningful and positive

## Working capital
- DSO = Average accounts receivable / Credit sales × days
- DPO = Average accounts payable / Credit purchases × days
- DIO = Average inventory / COGS × days
- Cash conversion cycle = DSO + DIO - DPO

## Cash and runway
- Net burn = Cash operating outflows - Cash operating inflows for the chosen period
- Monthly burn = Normalised monthly net burn
- Runway = Available cash / Monthly net burn
- If cash flow is positive, do not report a finite runway unless modelling a downside case.

## SaaS / subscription metrics
- CAC = Sales and marketing acquisition cost / New customers acquired
- ARPU = Recurring revenue / Average active customers
- Gross revenue churn % = Lost recurring revenue / Opening recurring revenue
- Net revenue retention = (Opening recurring revenue - churn - contraction + expansion) / Opening recurring revenue
- LTV must state the exact formula used. Do not mix gross-margin-adjusted and unadjusted definitions.

## Investment appraisal
- NPV = Sum of discounted future cash flows - Initial investment
- IRR = Discount rate at which NPV = 0
- Payback period = Time required for cumulative cash inflows to recover initial investment

## Valuation
- Enterprise value = Equity value + Debt + Preferred equity + Minority interest - Cash
- Equity value = Enterprise value - Debt - Preferred equity - Minority interest + Cash
- DCF assumptions must explicitly show forecast horizon, discount rate, terminal growth or exit multiple, and treatment of debt/cash.

## General rules
- Preserve source currency.
- Do not mix monthly and annual figures.
- Use weighted averages where timing materially matters.
- Avoid dividing by immaterial, negative, or non-comparable denominators without explanation.
- Reconcile totals before analysis.
